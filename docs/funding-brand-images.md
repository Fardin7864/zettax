- bKash: [bKash logo image on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bkash.webp).

These are third-party trademarks. Displaying a service logo identifies the method only; it does not imply an endorsement, partnership, or a live transfer. The current virtual-funding warning remains visible where applicable.
